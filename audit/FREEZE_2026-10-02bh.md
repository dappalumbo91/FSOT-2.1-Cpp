## BH-1 SU(2) constants

lbar_1 and lbar_2 are the D-wave sum rule of Pennington and Portoles, hep-ph/9409426, equations (16), (24) and (27), at lambda = 1.

The rho is the frozen KSRF resonance. g = 2 pi, m_rho^2 = 8 pi^2 F^2, and the width is Gamma = (pi m_rho / 12) (1 - 4 M_pi^2 / m_rho^2)^(3/2). F is the chiral-limit decay constant M_Q sqrt(N_c) / (2 pi), with M_Q = m_p / 3 and N_c = 3. M_pi is the charged-pion leaf.

The linear system is

lbar_1 + 4 lbar_2 - 53/8 = 1440 pi^3 F^4 a_2^0

-lbar_1 + lbar_2 + 97/120 = 288 pi^3 F^4 b_1^1

with a_2^0 and b_1^1 the rho integrals in equations (16) and (24).

The comparison is Colangelo, Gasser and Leutwyler, Nucl. Phys. B603 (2001) 125: lbar_1 = -0.4 ± 0.6 and lbar_2 = 4.3 ± 0.1. The score is z only.
